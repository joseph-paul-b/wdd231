NP Membership Benefits

Free membership for nonprofit organizations serving the Lagos community.

  • Free basic listing in the Lagos Business Connect online directory
  • Invitations to community networking events
  • Opportunities to connect with local businesses

Bronze Membership Benefits

For small businesses looking to build visibility and local connections.

  • All NP membership benefits
  • Business listing in the printed Lagos business directory
  • Invitations to quarterly networking mixers in Victoria Island
  • Access to selected business workshops

Silver Membership Benefits

For growing businesses ready to reach more customers and build stronger connections.

  • All Bronze membership benefits
  • Two free workshop seats per year
  • Business spotlight in the chamber's monthly newsletter
  • Priority invitations to selected networking events

Gold Membership Benefits

For established businesses seeking greater exposure and valuable business partnerships.

  • All Silver membership benefits
  • Featured business listing on the chamber homepage
  • Priority business introductions
  • Recognition at selected chamber events
  • Four free workshop seats per year
